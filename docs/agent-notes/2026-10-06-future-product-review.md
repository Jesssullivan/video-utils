## Decisions to retain

**Human issues:** preserve “known melodic/rhythm issue at xx.xx” immediately
through today's source-bound note route, with its exact supplied wording.
The proposed v2 separates semantic class, `operator_assertion` basis,
operator certainty and review state. Display **USER REPORTED**, retaining null
detector confidence/reference when absent. Recording this assertion requires
no invented detector confirmation or additional score approval. A point stays
a point; an audition window and label dwell are presentation choices. Native
sample-accurate storage is a transport goal, not proof a human-supplied boundary
is accurate to one sample. Unknown clock or imprecise time retains uncertainty.

**Fullness:** protect theoretical C1 near 32.7 Hz and harmonics, but do not
equate “more energy below 80 Hz” with better guitar preservation: fan and
musical lows overlap in the mono mixture. Inspect source→pure-denoise→tone
bundle→encoded delivery at common audition gains, including residue and
chugs/tapping/sustain. Isolate the existing 300 Hz cut and 2.2 kHz boost before
defaulting to a bass shelf. The operator's audible preference is an essential
gate. A new paired room/phone capture can qualify a reference; absent capture
response, EQ cannot establish recovery of the in-room amplifier sound. Artist
full-band spectra remain qualitative context.

**Reliability:** proposed p95 response, phase freshness and cancellation
numbers are useful engineering targets under named hardware/clip/load conditions.
They are not measured service availability or an external SLA. Durable enqueue,
restart, fenced publication and cancellation need actual failure receipts;
4–6-hour prototype estimates should not automatically claim the backend's
entire restart/load qualification matrix. Report failed requests, denominator,
warm/cold state and worker class. Never promise realtime turnaround from a
single completed offline render or a watchdog deadline.
