## Frozen audit targets

- Annotations: valid source-clock write/read, source/manifest mismatches, stale revision, replay idempotency versus conflicting replay, invalid/nonfinite/bool span, out-of-bounds/reversed span, unknown kind/basis/status, detector confirmation forbidden from a user report, existing v1 read compatibility. Record exact store bytes before and after every refusal. An empty new store must remain absent after a refused write.
- UI: visibly different user assertions and detector hypotheses; unknown values remain unknown; source-clock seeking/loop bounds; desktop and 390px walkthrough receipts; keyboard workflow; saved annotation must read back through the same bound source.
- Corpus: take-family groups and all derivatives stay in one split; identical source/hash aliases cannot escape grouping; invalid/unknown splits rejected; observed absence remains separate from unknown and unreviewed coverage; prior sparse C1 octave errors remain available with original denominator/provenance.
- Phrases: freeze baseline plus at most two candidate arms before results; heldout truth cannot enter proposal construction; independently recompute pair IoU matching and endpoint errors from raw proposals/truth, with separate true positives/false positives/false negatives, negatives and excluded/unknown cases. Empty positive predictions cannot count as perfect accuracy. Failed positives cannot justify default adoption.
- Low register: independent arithmetic from saved known-component arrays, separating isolated and collision fixtures. Recompute retention/error, onset/attack and sustain/tail windows, residual noise, clipping and exact denominators. Unknown/missing support does not become zero error. Fixture results cannot establish real mixture recovery or listening acceptance.
- Admission: every new product primitive has a closed typed schema and a matching intent/knobs/evidence skill; old 30 tools retain compatibility. Experimental helper qualification cannot masquerade as an advertised tool.
- Source protection: record accepted source/master/Desktop hashes before and after the bounded sprint; no worker may change accepted media or default detector/master merely to pass a metric.

## Independent evidence policy

Tests use generated inert metadata and temporary directories. Load exact owner files by absolute path where necessary, without edits. Record SHA-256 before/after targeted tests. Numerical validation reads sealed arrays and metadata only; no rerunning expensive inference to agree with a result. A reproduced defect is sent to its owner, and the fix is independently reproduced against the final frozen bytes. PASS, FAIL and unknown are separate outcomes. Root performs the combined post-merge suite.
