## Choices for review

- The job record is `acceptance.json` beside `job.json`. Writing into `job.json`
  would break `check_shape`, change the job digest and trip the private-terms
  binding of a guarded intake.
- A stage manifest is content addressed, so the run-side record is the recorded
  `graph.json` and the `accepted` field of `plan` and `run`.
- `vrm_badge_decision` refuses `vrm_badge_undecided` until a ruling exists.
  The design closes it only "once ruled".
- `accept` checks the id's form. The caller resolves the id to an Operator accept
  in Softproxy. No MCP tool is drafted, so an agent cannot close a gate through
  a hook.
