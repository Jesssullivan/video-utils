## Admission findings

Initial candidate requires amendments before numerical admission:

1. The runner populates FFMPEG/FFPROBE from an historical receipt but performs
   no bounded executable/version preflight. Resolve and record those actual
   binaries before generation/discovery release; retain the closed worker ENV.
2. Timeout cleanup signals only the immediate child. The child can spawn
   FFmpeg, whose timeout exceeds the controller's case allowance. Use a new
   owned session/group and inspect/clean that group, including descendants
   after leader exit, with R-N11 ownership and live-state receipts.
3. The 600-second timer starts after setup and lacks scoring/sealing checks.
   Cover setup, child waits and evaluation/receipt completion with the same
   bounded budget, retaining failed partial receipts and truthful truth-opened
   state. Small finite metrics do not substitute for that promised deadline.
4. The imported metric/JSON dependency `scripts/phrase_evaluate.py` is absent
   from the freeze. Its current SHA256 is
   `3e503de277b2fd233fe802595a09a655c89669b0eb820f228e677c1b94b828a5`.
   Bind it and recheck source/settings/result hashes before opening truth and
   before sealing evaluation. Otherwise a frozen runner can execute mutable
   matching semantics outside its recorded provenance.

These findings were sent to root and the implementation owner before any
numerical release. They are historical findings on the initial candidate;
the final readback below closes them. No quality result or default adoption
follows from either source checkpoint.
