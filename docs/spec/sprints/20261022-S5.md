Pipeline shape: input adapters (plain text, a markdown manuscript, SRT/VTT, condense `words.json`, `SPEAKER:`
dialog) produce a script IR. Then come a pinned lexical pass, spoken normalisation from a Dhall pronunciation table,
and a chunker of 300 codepoints or fewer. Next is an OpenAI-style `/v1/audio/speech` client with `ref_audio` and
`ref_text`, and per-chunk whisper WER and speaker-similarity gates with best-of-3. The audio is then loudness-set to
−16 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP and fitted to SRT durations: first the engine's `speed`, then `atempo` within ±8%; overruns are
flagged, never truncated. Outputs are WAV/FLAC, SRT, a narration EDL per `docs/spec/TIMELINE_CONTRACT.md`, and
`manifest.json` with `synthetic: true`. Narrate output lives outside `artifacts/runs/`, like condense. Source media is
never time-stretched.

## N0 acceptance

All of these hold, each as its own state:

- `just narrate-render` on a public manuscript page, with the picked local engine, writes a WAV, an SRT and a
  manifest.
- Every chunk passes its WER and speaker-similarity gates, and loudness is within ±0.5 LU of target.
- The consent refusal and the confidential-job refusal for hosted engines are tested.
- The `narrate_*` tools are admitted and listed by `just mcp`.
- The operator's blind A/B listening receipt is recorded. Source checks passing does not make it listening
  acceptance.
