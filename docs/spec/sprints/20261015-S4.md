## Product goals

1. One toolkit, two lanes. The guitar lane and the talk lane (`./condense`) share one CLI, one tool contract and
   one timeline contract (`docs/spec/TIMELINE_CONTRACT.md`).
2. Every operation is a typed tool with a skill. Every run is hash-bound and checked by `verify-run`.
3. A recorded talk becomes a publishable condensed video with one command. No word is dropped. Sync and loudness
   are measured on real recordings.
4. The guitar lane keeps the 32 Hz fundamental. Claims are measurements with confidence. No note-correctness verdict
   without an approved reference.
5. The public tree holds no real media, host names or personal paths. Models are pinned by hash. Main stays green.
